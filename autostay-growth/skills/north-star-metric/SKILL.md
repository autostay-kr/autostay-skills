# North Star Metric

Identify a North Star Metric and 3-5 Input Metrics that form a metrics constellation for Autostay. Pre-classified as a Transaction Game with subscription-specific NSM candidates and input metrics.

## Domain Context

**Autostay** — O2O 세차 구독 서비스
- 비즈니스 모델: 월간/연간 구독으로 세차 서비스 제공
- 고객: 차량 소유자 (구독자)
- 공급: 파트너 세차장 네트워크
- 핵심 지표: MRR, 구독자 수, Churn Rate, LTV, NPS
- O2O 루프: 온라인 예약 → 오프라인 세차 → 디지털 피드백 루프

NSM is **NOT**: multiple metrics, a revenue/LTV metric (must be customer-centric), an OKR (that's a goal-setting technique), or a strategy (but choosing the right NSM is a strategic choice).

NSM **IS**: a single, customer-centric KPI that reflects the value customers get from the product and serves as a leading indicator of long-term business success. You can use Key Results (OKRs) to express expected change in NSM.

## Pre-Classification: Transaction Game

Autostay is a **Transaction Game** business. Revenue comes from recurring transactions (subscriptions) between car owners and the car wash service network. The core value loop is: subscribe → book wash → receive service → renew.

Other game types for reference:
- **Attention Game**: How much time do customers spend using your product? (Examples: Facebook, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok)
- **Transaction Game**: How many transactions occur between customers and your platform? (Examples: Amazon, Uber, Airbnb, PayPal, **Autostay**)
- **Productivity Game**: How efficiently can someone complete their work or achieve their goals? (Examples: Canva, Dropbox, Loom, Notion)

## Recommended NSM Candidates

For Autostay, consider these three North Star Metric candidates:

1. **Monthly Active Subscribers** — Number of subscribers who completed at least one wash booking in the current month. Reflects active engagement with the subscription value.
2. **Total Washes Completed** — Total number of car washes delivered per month across all subscribers. Directly measures value delivery volume.
3. **Subscription Renewal Rate** — Percentage of subscribers who renew at the end of their billing cycle (monthly or annual). Captures sustained satisfaction and retention.

## Pre-Filled Input Metrics

Regardless of which NSM is chosen, these input metrics drive Autostay's North Star:

| Input Metric | Description | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| New Subscriber Signups | Number of new paid subscriptions started per month | Growth |
| First Wash Completion Rate | % of new subscribers who complete their first wash within 7 days | Onboarding |
| Average Washes per Subscriber | Mean number of washes booked per active subscriber per month | Product |
| Partner Coverage Score | % of subscribers within 15-min drive of a partner wash center | Operations |
| NPS / Satisfaction Score | Post-wash customer satisfaction rating | Quality |

## Prompt

You are a metrics strategist specializing in North Star metrics and growth measurement frameworks for subscription businesses.

Given the following business context: $ARGUMENTS

Note: Autostay is pre-classified as a **Transaction Game** business. Use the recommended NSM candidates and input metrics above as a starting point, then refine based on the user's specific context.

**Step 1: Validate the Business Game Classification**
Confirm that Transaction Game is correct for the user's context, or adjust if the business has evolved.

**Step 2: Identify the North Star Metric**
Evaluate the three recommended candidates (Monthly Active Subscribers, Total Washes Completed, Subscription Renewal Rate) and suggest the best fit. Validate against all seven criteria:

1. **Easy to Understand**: Clear definition that everyone in the organization comprehends
2. **Customer-Centric**: Reflects value delivered to customers, not just revenue or activity
3. **Sustainable Value**: Indicates habits and long-term customer engagement
4. **Vision Alignment**: Represents meaningful progress toward the company's vision and mission
5. **Quantitative**: Measurable with clear, numeric tracking
6. **Actionable**: Teams can directly influence it through product, marketing, and operational changes
7. **Leading Indicator**: Predicts future business success and revenue growth

**Step 3: Refine Input Metrics**
Start from the pre-filled input metrics table above and adjust based on the user's current stage, data availability, and team structure. Each input metric should:
- Be easier to move in the short term
- Directly contribute to the North Star outcome
- Help identify where optimization efforts should focus

## Tips for Best Results

- Share Autostay's current stage (early, growth, mature)
- Provide current metrics you're tracking and their values
- Mention key customer segments (plan types, regions, car types)
- Describe the primary value proposition as perceived by subscribers
- Share any known issues (high churn, low first-wash rate, etc.)
