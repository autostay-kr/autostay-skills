# Autostay — Product Marketing Context

## Company Overview

- **Company**: Autostay (오토스테이)
- **Industry**: O2O Car Wash Subscription Service
- **Market**: South Korea (primarily Seoul metropolitan area, expanding to other regions)
- **Business Model**: Monthly subscription — subscribers visit partner car wash shops for unlimited washes
- **Two-sided Platform**: Subscribers (B2C) + Partner car wash shops (B2B)

## Product

- **Core Offering**: Monthly car wash subscription plans (무제한 세차 구독)
- **How it Works**: Subscribe → Visit any partner shop → Get your car washed → No per-visit payment
- **Value Proposition (Subscriber)**: Predictable monthly cost, unlimited car washes, no hassle
- **Value Proposition (Partner Shop)**: Steady recurring revenue, new customer acquisition, reduced marketing costs
- **Key Differentiator**: Korea's first O2O car wash subscription platform connecting subscribers with local car wash shops

## Target Audiences

### Subscribers (B2C)
- Car owners in Korea who wash their cars regularly (2+ times/month)
- Value convenience and cost savings
- Age: 25-55, primarily urban/suburban
- Pain points: Expensive per-wash costs, inconsistent quality, inconvenient locations
- Language: Korean (한국어)

### Partner Car Wash Shops (B2B)
- Independent and franchise car wash shops
- Want steady customer flow and predictable revenue
- Pain points: Seasonal demand fluctuation, high customer acquisition cost, empty time slots
- Decision makers: Shop owners/managers

## Pricing & Plans
- Multiple subscription tiers (basic to premium)
- Monthly billing (구독 결제)
- Currency: KRW (원)
- No per-visit charges for subscribers

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Language**: Korean (한국어) — all customer-facing copy should be in Korean
- **Tone**: Friendly, trustworthy, practical (not overly corporate or playful)
- **Style**: Clear and direct, emphasizing value and convenience
- **Avoid**: Overly technical jargon, aggressive sales tactics, exaggerated claims

## Key Metrics
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- Subscriber count & growth rate
- Churn rate (voluntary + involuntary)
- Partner shop count & coverage area
- Wash frequency per subscriber
- NPS (Net Promoter Score)
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- LTV (Lifetime Value)

## Competitive Landscape
- Traditional per-visit car wash (일반 세차장)
- Self-service car wash (셀프 세차장)
- Home/mobile car wash services (출장 세차)
- Other subscription-based car care services

## Channels
- App (primary touchpoint for subscribers)
- Website
- Social media (Instagram, KakaoTalk, Naver Blog)
- Partner shop in-store materials
- Naver/Google search (SEO)
- Paid ads (Naver Ads, Google Ads, Meta Ads)

## Seasonality
- **Peak**: Spring (yellow dust/황사), rainy season aftermath, winter (salt/road grime)
- **Low**: Mid-summer (heavy rain makes washes feel pointless)
- Seasonal messaging should adjust accordingly

## Regulatory & Cultural Notes
- Korean consumer protection laws apply to subscription cancellation
- Easy cancellation is legally required and brand-aligned
- Korean customers value trust, social proof, and reviews (Naver reviews, KakaoTalk recommendations)
- Kakao ecosystem integration is important (KakaoTalk notifications, Kakao Pay)
