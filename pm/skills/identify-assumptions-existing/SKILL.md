## Identify Assumptions (Autostay)

Devil's advocate analysis to surface risky assumptions across seven risk areas, including O2O-specific categories for Autostay's car wash subscription service.

### Domain Context

**Autostay** — O2O 세차 구독 서비스
- 비즈니스 모델: 월간/연간 구독으로 세차 서비스 제공
- 고객: 차량 소유자 (구독자)
- 공급: 파트너 세차장 네트워크
- 핵심 지표: MRR, 구독자 수, Churn Rate, LTV, NPS
- O2O 루프: 온라인 예약 → 오프라인 세차 → 디지털 피드백 루프

### Context

You are stress-testing a feature idea for **$ARGUMENTS** in the context of Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service.

If the user provides files (designs, PRDs, research), read them first.

### Instructions

The user will describe their product area, objective, market segment, and feature idea. Work through these steps:

1. **Think from three perspectives** about why this feature might fail:
   - **Product Manager perspective**: Business viability, market fit, strategic alignment, subscription model impact
   - **Designer perspective**: Usability, user experience, adoption barriers across online and offline touchpoints
   - **Engineer perspective**: Technical feasibility, performance, integration challenges, O2O system complexity

2. **Identify assumptions across seven risk areas**:

   **Core Risk Categories:**
   - **Value**: Will it create value for subscribers? Does it solve a real problem for car owners?
   - **Usability**: Will subscribers figure out how to use it? Is the learning curve acceptable across both app and offline experience?
   - **Viability**: Can marketing, sales, finance, and legal support it? Does it work within the subscription pricing model?
   - **Feasibility**: Can it be built with existing technology? Are there integration risks with partner systems?

   **O2O Risk Categories (Autostay-specific):**
   - **파트너 확보 (Partner Acquisition)**: Will enough partner car wash businesses adopt this? Does it require partner behavior change? What is the onboarding burden?
   - **서비스 품질 (Service Quality)**: Can consistent quality be maintained across different partner locations? How does this feature affect the offline service delivery?
   - **지역 커버리지 (Geographic Coverage)**: Does this feature work across all service areas? Does it create urban/suburban imbalance? Are there location-dependent constraints?

3. **For each assumption**, note:
   - What specifically could go wrong
   - How confident you are (High/Medium/Low)
   - Suggested way to test it
   - Which Autostay metric it most impacts (MRR, Churn Rate, LTV, NPS)

Think step by step. Be thorough but constructive — the goal is to strengthen the idea, not kill it.
