## Competitive Battlecard — Autostay

Create a concise, sales-ready battlecard for Autostay against a specific competitor in the car wash market.

### Domain Context

**Autostay** — O2O car wash subscription service
- Business model: Monthly/annual subscriptions for car wash services
- Customers: Vehicle owners (subscribers)
- Supply: Partner car wash network
- Key metrics: MRR, subscriber count, Churn Rate, LTV, NPS
- O2O loop: Online booking -> Offline car wash -> Digital feedback loop

### Context

You are creating a competitive battlecard for Autostay vs **$ARGUMENTS**.

Use web search to research the competitor's current product, pricing, positioning, and recent changes. If the user provides files (feature lists, win/loss data, customer feedback), read them first.

### Car Wash Competitor Categories

When analyzing competitors, categorize them into one of these car wash industry segments:

| Category | Description | Examples | Key Strengths | Key Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Self-Service Car Wash (셀프 세차장)** | Coin-operated or pay-per-use self-wash bays | Local self-wash stations | Low cost, immediate availability | Time-consuming, physical labor, weather-dependent |
| **Mobile/Visit Car Wash (출장 세차)** | On-demand car wash that comes to the customer | Various mobile wash services | Extreme convenience, no travel needed | Expensive per wash, inconsistent quality, scheduling friction |
| **Other Car Wash Subscription Services (다른 세차 구독 서비스)** | Competing subscription-based car wash platforms | Emerging subscription services | Similar model, established user base | Direct competitors — differentiation is critical |
| **Automated Car Wash Machines (자동 세차기)** | Drive-through automatic car wash | Gas station car washes, standalone automated facilities | Fast, cheap, consistent | Potential paint damage, no interior cleaning, impersonal |
