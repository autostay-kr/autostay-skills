# /strategy -- Autostay Product Strategy

Build a complete product strategy document for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service. Combines the Product Strategy Canvas with subscription pricing design to produce a unified strategic plan.

## Invocation

```
/strategy Autostay's expansion into Busan market
/strategy Launching a premium detailing subscription tier
/strategy [upload existing strategy doc, pitch deck, or business plan]
/strategy                    # asks about your strategic focus
```

## Workflow

### Step 1: Understand the Strategic Context

Ask:
- What is the strategic focus? (new market, new tier, overall strategy, pivot, annual planning)
- What stage is Autostay in for this area? (idea, pilot, growth, mature)
- What's the current subscriber base and MRR?
- What triggered the need for a strategy document? (expansion, competitive pressure, fundraise, annual planning)
- Current partner network status and geographic coverage?

### Step 2: Build the Strategy Canvas

Apply the **product-strategy** and **product-vision** skills:

Work through all 9 sections of the Strategy Canvas, contextualized for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription:

1. **Vision**: Inspiring north star for Autostay — how we transform car care for every vehicle owner
2. **Target Segments**: Which car owner segments we serve (and which we don't)
3. **Pain Points & Value**: Car care problems we solve and the subscription value we create
4. **Value Propositions**: JTBD-framed value for each segment (busy professionals, multi-car families, premium car owners)
5. **Strategic Trade-offs**: What we choose NOT to do (own facilities? B2B only? nationwide from day one?)
6. **Key Metrics**: MRR, churn rate, LTV, NPS, partner utilization, booking completion rate
7. **Growth Engine**: O2O flywheel — subscribers -> partner demand -> better coverage -> more subscribers
8. **Core Capabilities**: Partner management platform, booking system, quality assurance, customer app
9. **Defensibility**: Partner network density, subscriber data, geographic network effects, switching costs

Plus Autostay-specific strategy sections:
- **Partner Network Strategy**: Recruitment, quality standards, revenue share, density targets
- **Geographic Expansion**: City selection, minimum viable supply density, launch sequence
- **Subscription Model Design**: Tier structure, monthly vs annual, add-ons, family plans

For each section, provide specific content — not generic advice.

### Step 3: Integrate Pricing Strategy

Apply the **pricing-strategy** skill:

- Subscription tier design (Basic / Standard / Premium)
- Monthly vs annual pricing with appropriate discounts
- Per-wash economics and partner revenue share
- Add-on services and upsell paths
- Corporate/fleet pricing

## Notes

- A good strategy is more about what you say NO to than what you say YES to — push hard on trade-offs
- Vision should be emotional and memorable, not a corporate statement
- For O2O businesses, supply-side strategy (partner network) is as critical as demand-side strategy (subscribers)
- Defensibility in O2O comes from geographic density — being "everywhere" in a region beats being "somewhere" in many regions
- If the product is early-stage, some sections will be hypotheses — label them as such
- Strategy should fit on one page for executives — offer a condensed version
