# autostay-toolkit

Autostay utility skills: partner car wash onboarding, service quality review, and grammar checking. Essential tools for managing Autostay's O2O car wash subscription partner network and maintaining service quality.

## Skills (3)

- **grammar-check** — Identify grammar, logical, and flow errors in text and suggest targeted fixes without rewriting the entire text.
- **partner-onboarding** — Generate partner car wash shop onboarding materials — checklists, SLA templates, quality standards, and training guides for Autostay's partner network.
- **service-quality-review** — Analyze car wash service quality from subscriber feedback and partner performance data — quality scoring, issue detection, and improvement plans for Autostay.

## Commands (1)

- `/autostay-toolkit:review-feedback` — Analyze subscriber and partner feedback — categorize issues, assess quality, and generate improvement plans.

## Dependencies

- `autostay-discovery` — The `/review-feedback` command chains the `analyze-feature-requests` skill from the `autostay-discovery` plugin for feedback categorization.
