## Workflow

### Step 1: Identify the Matchup

Ask:
- Which specific competitor or competitor category?
- Who is the typical car owner choosing between Autostay and this competitor?
- What context triggers the comparison? (cost concerns, convenience, quality, habit)
- Do you have customer feedback or win/loss data?
- What region is this comparison relevant for?

### Step 2: Research the Competitor

Apply the **competitive-battlecard** skill with web research:

- Current service offerings and coverage
- Pricing model and published pricing
- Target market and positioning
- Customer reviews (Naver, Kakao, app stores, car communities like Bobaedream)
- Known strengths and weaknesses
- Recent changes or expansions

## Notes

- Car wash competition is hyper-local — a battlecard for Seoul may not apply to Busan
- The biggest competitor is often inertia: "I'll wash it myself this weekend" — address the habit, not just the competitor
- Never trash the competitor — position on Autostay's strengths (convenience, consistency, subscription value)
- For self-wash and automated wash, the real comparison is time + quality, not just cost
- For mobile wash, the real comparison is reliability + cost, not just convenience
- Win/loss data from actual subscriber conversations is worth 10x any analysis — encourage the team to track it
- Keep it to one page equivalent — team members need to reference this quickly
