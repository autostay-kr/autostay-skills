# autostay-strategy

Autostay product strategy skills: subscription business strategy, pricing plan design, regional expansion GTM, and competitive analysis for O2O car wash subscription service.

## Skills (7)

- **product-vision** — Brainstorm an inspiring, achievable, and emotional product vision that motivates teams and aligns stakeholders.
- **beachhead-segment** — Identify the first beachhead market segment for a product launch, evaluating segments against burning pain, willingness to pay, winnable market share, and referral potential.
- **product-strategy** — Create a comprehensive product strategy for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service using the 9-section Product Strategy Canvas with O2O-specific sections: Partner Network Strategy, Geographic Expansion, and Subscription Model Design.
- **pricing-strategy** — Design subscription pricing strategies for Autostay — monthly vs annual plans, tier structure, add-ons, per-wash economics, bundle pricing, family plans, and partner revenue share modeling.
- **lean-canvas** — Generate a Lean Canvas for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service with pre-filled examples for problem, solution, UVP, and other sections.
- **gtm-strategy** — Create a go-to-market strategy for Autostay covering marketing channels, messaging, regional expansion, partner recruitment, and city-by-city launch planning.
- **competitive-battlecard** — Create competitive battlecards for Autostay comparing against car wash competitor categories: self-wash stations, mobile wash, automated car wash, and other subscription services.

## Commands (4)

- `/autostay-strategy:strategy` — Create a comprehensive product strategy for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service — strategy canvas, vision, and subscription pricing in one document.
- `/autostay-strategy:pricing` — Design subscription pricing for Autostay — plan tiers, monthly vs annual, per-wash economics, add-ons, family plans, and partner revenue share.
- `/autostay-strategy:plan-expansion` — Plan regional expansion for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service — market selection, partner recruitment, and launch playbook.
- `/autostay-strategy:battlecard` — Create a competitive battlecard for Autostay against car wash industry competitors — self-wash, mobile wash, automated wash, and other subscription services.

## Domain Context

**Autostay** — O2O car wash subscription service
- Business model: Monthly/annual subscriptions for car wash services
- Customers: Vehicle owners (subscribers)
- Supply: Partner car wash network
- Key metrics: MRR, subscriber count, Churn Rate, LTV, NPS
- O2O loop: Online booking -> Offline car wash -> Digital feedback loop
