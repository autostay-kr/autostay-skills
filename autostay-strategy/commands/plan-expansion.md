# /plan-expansion -- Autostay Regional Expansion

Plan a city-by-city regional expansion for Autostay's O2O car wash subscription service. Combines GTM strategy, beachhead segment analysis, and competitive battlecards to produce a complete expansion playbook for a target city or region.

## Invocation

```
/plan-expansion Busan metropolitan area
/plan-expansion Gyeonggi-do satellite cities (Suwon, Yongin, Seongnam)
/plan-expansion Daejeon as second city after Seoul
/plan-expansion [upload market research or partner data]
```

## Workflow

### Step 1: Understand the Expansion Target

Ask:
- What city or region are you expanding to?
- What is Autostay's current coverage? (which cities/districts are already live)
- How many subscribers and partners do you have currently?
- What's the timeline and budget for this expansion?
- Do you have any existing partners or contacts in the target region?
- What triggered this expansion? (subscriber demand, investor expectation, competitive pressure)

### Step 2: Evaluate Market Attractiveness

Assess the target region against selection criteria:

| Criterion | Weight | How to Evaluate |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Vehicle density | High | Registered vehicles per district, car ownership rate |
| Car wash market size | High | Number of existing car washes, estimated market spend |
| Partner availability | Critical | Car wash businesses willing to partner, quality distribution |
| Competitive intensity | Medium | Existing subscription/O2O car wash presence |
| Demographic fit | Medium | Income levels, age distribution, urbanization |
| Infrastructure | Low | App penetration, digital payment adoption |

### Step 3: Define Beachhead Segment for the Region

Apply the **beachhead-segment** skill:

Evaluate potential beachhead segments within the target region:
- Which neighborhoods/districts to launch first?
- Which car owner segments have the most burning pain?
- Where is the highest concentration of potential subscribers?
- Which area has the best partner car wash coverage?
- Where can you achieve minimum viable supply density fastest?

Scoring criteria:
- Burning pain (need for convenient car wash)
- Willingness to pay (income and car value in the area)
- Winnable share (competitive landscape in the district)
- Referral potential (community density, apartment complexes, car communities)

### Step 4: Build Partner Recruitment Plan

Supply-side activation is critical before demand-side marketing:

1. **Partner Mapping**: Identify all car wash businesses in the target region
   - Self-service wash stations
   - Hand wash shops
   - Detailing studios
   - Mobile wash operators

2. **Partner Value Proposition**: Why should a car wash join Autostay?
   - Guaranteed subscriber traffic and bookings
   - Digital booking management (reduce phone calls, walk-in uncertainty)
   - Marketing support and brand association
   - Data insights on customer preferences
   - Revenue stability through subscription volume

3. **Recruitment Targets**:
   - Minimum partners needed for launch: [calculate based on supply density target]
   - Partner distribution by district: [map to subscriber density]
   - Mix of partner types: [hand wash, detailing, mobile]

4. **Onboarding Process**:
   - Quality audit and certification
   - System integration and training
   - Pricing and payout agreement
   - Test period and feedback loop

### Step 5: Competitive Landscape Analysis

Apply the **competitive-battlecard** skill:

Analyze the competitive environment in the target region:
- Existing car wash providers (by category: self-service, automated, mobile, subscription)
- Pricing benchmarks in the local market
- Customer satisfaction gaps (from reviews, community posts)
- Potential competitive response to Autostay's entry
- Create battlecards for top 2-3 local competitors

### Step 6: Build Regional GTM Strategy

Apply the **gtm-strategy** skill:

Design the regional launch plan with O2O-specific channels:

**Online Channels**:
- Local search optimization (Naver Place, Kakao Map)
- Regional social media targeting
- Local car owner communities and forums
- App store promotion targeting the region

**Offline/O2O Channels**:
- Apartment complex partnerships in target districts
- Partner car wash signage and QR codes
- Local car dealership partnerships
- Gas station and parking lot partnerships
- Local car meets and events

**Referral Channels**:
- Subscriber referral incentives
- Partner-to-subscriber referral program
- Corporate partnerships with local employers

## Notes

- For O2O businesses, supply-side (partners) must be secured BEFORE demand-side (subscribers) — plan partner recruitment weeks before marketing launch
- Minimum viable supply density is the single most important pre-launch metric — subscribers who can't find a nearby partner will churn immediately
- Start with the smallest winnable area (one district) and expand outward — geographic density beats geographic breadth
- Partner quality in the first region sets the standard for all future expansions — don't rush quality for speed
- Budget for higher CAC in new regions — you don't have word-of-mouth yet
- Local competitive dynamics vary significantly by region — don't assume Seoul patterns apply elsewhere
- Weather and seasonal patterns affect car wash demand differently by region
